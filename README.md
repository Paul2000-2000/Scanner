📦 Transport Company Stock & Sales Management System
This project is a comprehensive Stock and Sales Management System designed for transport companies, with a focus on tracking inventory, managing product inputs and outputs, and handling sales efficiently.

🔧 Key Features
Inventory Tracking: Manage and monitor stock levels in real time.

Sales Management: Record and analyze all sales transactions.

Input/Output Management: Log all incoming and outgoing items with full traceability.

User-Friendly Interface: Simple, intuitive UI for smooth operation by company staff.

🚚 Designed for Transport Companies — Adaptable for Others
While this system is specifically tailored for the needs of transport companies, it is highly flexible and adaptable. Businesses operating in logistics, warehousing, delivery, or distribution can easily customize and implement this solution for their workflows.

⚙️ Customization Ready
The system architecture is designed to be easily extendable and customizable to meet unique business requirements. Whether you're handling complex logistics or straightforward inventory tracking, this project can be adapted to fit your company’s structure.
